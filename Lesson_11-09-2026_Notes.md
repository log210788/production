## 3. Grammar & Natural Phrasing Polish
- ***"I don't want he to start..."*** → ***"I don't want him to start..."*** (use object pronoun *him* after *want*).
- ***"In my born city"*** → ***"In my hometown"*** / ***"In the city where I was born."***
- ***"It is itchy" (about yourself)*** → ***"I'm really itchy"*** (use *I'm* for how you feel; *it's itchy* for the bite/skin itself).
- ***"He has forbidden to hit"*** → ***"He is forbidden from hitting"*** / ***"He's not allowed to hit."***
