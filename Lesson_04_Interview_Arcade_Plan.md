# 🎮 Fun Interview Practice Lesson — Bárbara G.
**Interactive Web App:** [lesson-04-interview-arcade.html](./lesson-04-interview-arcade.html)  
**Target:** High B2–C1 • Angular / TypeScript Developer (ex-Artistic Director & Goya-nominated Producer)  
**Format:** 50-Minute Game-Show Style Speaking Practice (zero-stress, high-energy!)

---

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Flow

### 1. Game 1: *The Confidence Buzzer* (00:00 – 12:00)
- **How to play:** Share your screen on Tab 1. Read the red **"Undersell Alert"** out loud.
- **Rule:** Before clicking any option, Barbara has **10 seconds** to say a stronger, zero-apology version *out loud*. Then she picks the best Senior Reframe on screen to score points + confetti.
- **Coach cue:** Whenever she says *"just"*, *"only"*, or *"sorry"*, hit the buzzer playfully and make her re-run the sentence!

### 2. Game 2: *Spin & Power Word Roulette* (12:00 – 27:00)
- **How to play:** Click **SPIN** on the wheel! It lands on a question (including fun curveballs like *"Paella Code"*) AND deals a **Secret Power Word** (`trade-off`, `edge case`, `bottleneck`, `cross-functional`, `refactor`, `encapsulation`, `ownership`, `scalability`).
- **Rule:** Start the **90s Timer**. If she answers smoothly AND sneaks the Secret Power Word in naturally, click **`✅ Used It! (+200)`**.
- **Coach cue:** Remind her to use the **Hesitation Killers** at the bottom if she needs 2 seconds to think instead of saying *"umm"*.

### 3. Game 3: *STAR Speed-Run* (27:00 – 40:00)
- **Part A (3 mins):** *Fix the Rambling Candidate!* Have her click the 2 fluff lines to snap the bar into **S 15% • T 10% • A 50% • R 25%**.
- **Part B (10 mins):** *60-Second STAR Sprint!* Pick a prompt, hit **Start Sprint**, and watch the 4 phases light up (`0–9s S` → `9–15s T` → `15–45s A` → `45–60s R`). Do 2–3 rounds!

### 4. Game 4: *Hire or Pass?* Role Reversal (40:00 – 50:00)
- **How to play:** Put Barbara in **Louie's Hiring Director chair**. She reads 5 candidate quotes, votes **Hire (Green Flag)** or **Pass (Red Flag)**, and explains *why* in 15 seconds.
- **Why this works:** Judging other candidates' answers kills interview anxiety and makes senior vs. junior framing click instantly.
