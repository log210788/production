## 🎯 Core Shift from Lesson 04 → Lesson 05
In **Lesson 04**, Barbara trained *zero-apology reframes* and the *15/10/50/25 STAR split*.  
In **Lesson 05**, she faces the **second & final interview stages** where non-native devs usually freeze:
1. **Narrating code out loud** with accurate English punctuation (`colon`, `arrow function`, `generic`, `optional chaining`, `nullish coalescing`).
2. **Defending CV curveballs** (4-year family leave, leaving high-overtime production, post-internship transition, responsible AI usage).
3. **Giving a crisp 3-minute screen-share demo** of a real Angular/DAW project without rambling.
4. **Negotiating remote salary & healthy work-life boundaries** without underselling herself or sounding rigid.

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Teaching Flow

### 1. Stage 1: *Code Narrator* — Live PR Review (00:00 – 12:00)
*Open Tab 1 (`1. Code Narrator`) & paste Preply Canvas Block 1.*
- **Warm-up (2 mins):** Click through the 8 **Code Punctuation** chips (`:`, `;`, `=>`, `<T>`, `?:`, `?.`, `??`, `{}`) and have Barbara repeat them at natural speed (fixes the Lesson 01 *"two points"* → **colon** habit).
- **Live Narration (10 mins):** Go through **PR Reviews #1–#4**.
  - **Rule:** Barbara reads the buggy snippet and speaks her diagnosis + refactor **before** clicking **Reveal Fix & Model Script**.
  - **Award `+100 Spoken Win`** whenever she narrates continuously for 20+ seconds without stopping to apologize.

### 2. Stage 2: *Curveball Shield* — Tough Follow-Ups (12:00 – 25:00)
*Switch to Tab 2 (`2. Curveball Shield`) & paste Preply Canvas Block 2.*
- **How to run:** Pick 3–4 of the 6 curveballs (prioritize **#1 Career Gap**, **#2 Leaving Events/Hours**, and **#4 AI & Junior Hiring**).
- **Rule:** Start the **45s Timer**. Listen for two traps:
  1. **Over-explaining / apologizing** for her parenting gap.
  2. **Venting** about 26-hour overtime weeks in her old job (help her reframe to *seeking sustainable engineering craft*).
- Reveal **Defensive Trap vs. Senior Reframe** after each answer and have her re-run her favorite 2 sentences.

### 3. Stage 3: *3-Min Project Demo* — Screen-Share Walkthrough (25:00 – 38:00)
*Switch to Tab 3 (`3. 3-Min Project Demo`) & paste Preply Canvas Block 3.*
- **Setup (2 mins):** Ask Barbara to pick **Sopra Steria / Angular App** or **DAW Full-Stack Capstone** (she can actually share her screen or talk through it conceptually).
- **Live Run (11 mins):** Hit **Start 3-Min Demo**. Watch the 4 beats light up (`0:00–0:30 Problem` → `0:30–1:15 Architecture` → `1:15–2:15 Hardest Bug / TS` → `2:15–3:00 Next Sprint`).
- **Coach Cue:** Point to the **Anti-Freeze Connectors** panel on the right whenever she pauses between screens/files. Do 1 rough run + 1 tighter polish if time allows.

### 4. Stage 4: *Offer & Boundaries* — Compensation & Culture Fit (38:00 – 50:00)
*Switch to Tab 4 (`4. Offer & Boundaries`) & paste Preply Canvas Block 4.*
- **Part A — 3 Roleplay Scenarios (7 mins):** Run the Recruiter/Hiring Manager prompts (*Salary Expectations*, *Busy Releases & Availability*, *Start Date & Onboarding*). Have her speak an answer first, then pick the balanced option on screen.
- **Part B — Remote Culture Vetting (5 mins):** Have Barbara pick **2 questions** from the **Remote Culture Vetting Checklist** and ask them to Louie as if he's the Engineering Lead.
- **Homework:** Record a **2–3 minute screen-share video** (Loom or phone/webcam) walking through one Angular or DAW project using the 4-beat structure and at least 3 spoken TypeScript terms (`colon`, `generic`, `optional chaining`, `encapsulation`).

## 🛟 Pacing Rescue
- **If she gets stuck on code syntax in Stage 1:** Remind her she isn't being graded on memorizing syntax—she's practicing *explaining design intent* in English.
- **If she doesn't have a project open for Stage 3:** Use the **DAW Full-Stack Capstone** track conceptually—sketching *"Login → Form → API → DB"* verbally is great practice.
