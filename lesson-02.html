The 3 Unpacking Rooms & Preposition Landmarks

A visual overview of how spatial prepositions govern each room in the exercise:

1. Bedroom & Study 🛏️
  • • Flush to the wall: The window & desk
  • • Hang up / on the hanger: Flannel shirts & coats
  • • On the top / middle / bottom shelf: Books, figurines, diary
  • • On the bedside table: Alarm clock, small lamp
  • • In the bottom drawer: Folded underwear / knickers
  • • Above the bed: Cork pinboard with photos
2. En-Suite Bathroom 🚿
  • • On the towel rail: Bath and hand towels
  • • Under the sink: First aid kit (*botiquín*), extra soaps
  • • On the sink ledge: Toothbrush & toothpaste
  • • Inside the cabinet: Sanitary pads & tampons
  • • In the shower cubicle: Shower gel & shampoo
3. Kitchen & Dining 🍳
  • • In the cutlery drawer: Tablespoons, teaspoons, forks, knives
  • • On the worktop / counter: Cookie jar, toaster, chopping board
  • • Beside the sink: Scouring pad, washing-up liquid
  • • On the stove: Boiling pot / stockpot
  • • Hanging on oven handle: Clean tea towel
