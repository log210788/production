Part 1 • 13 Mins • Stop Memorizing Random Lists

The Tech Phrasal Verb Decoder: Read the Particle!

Spanish speakers already know formal English verbs (deploy, resolve, eliminate, postpone) because they come from Latin. In daily standups and Slack, native devs use Phrasal Verbs. The secret: group them by the particle (OUT, UP, OFF, DOWN), which holds 70% of the visual meaning!

1. [ OUT ] Expand / Eliminate

Imagine unrolling a carpet across a room, or smoothing wrinkles out flat.

roll out🔊
deploy gradually (desplegar)
iron out🔊
smooth out small bugs (pulir)
rule out🔊
discard/eliminate a cause (descartar)
2. [ UP ] Make Visible / Start

Imagine raising a red warning flag in the air, or an engine spinning to life.

flag up🔊
raise a risk early (señalar / avisar)
spin up🔊
launch a server/container fast
bring up🔊
mention a topic (sacar un tema)
3. [ OFF ] Launch / Checkpoint

Imagine kicking a football to start a match, or pushing a task off your desk.

kick off🔊
start a meeting/project (arrancar)
sign off on🔊
officially approve (dar el visto bueno)
put off🔊
postpone/delay (posponer)
4. [ DOWN ] Split / Funnel

Imagine breaking a big rock into small pieces, or funneling 10 suspects to 1.

break down🔊
split into small tasks (desglosar)
narrow down🔊
reduce possibilities (acotar)
turn down🔊
reject an offer/idea (rechazar)
🥪 The "Pronoun Sandwich" Rule (IT / THEM)

When you replace the noun with it or them, lock the pronoun IN THE MIDDLE: roll IT out • iron THEM out • put IT off • narrow IT down (3-word exception: sign off on IT)

Part 2 • 12 Mins • Zero-Ramble Architecture & Standup Updates

The 45-Second "BLUF" Daily Standup & Bug Triage

BLUF = Bottom Line Up Front. Instead of telling a long chronological story and losing your train of thought, hit the 3 lighted beats below in 45 seconds flat.

00:45
0:00 – 0:10 • Beat 1
1. Headline Status

"Quick update on [the feature/bug]—here is where we stand..."

0:10 – 0:30 • Beat 2
2. Ruled Out & Narrowed Down

"We ruled out X and narrowed it down to Y. I'm ironing it out now."

0:30 – 0:45 • Beat 3
3. Sign-Off / Rollout Ask

"To roll it out tomorrow, I just need [Team] to sign off on Z."
