## 05–18 min | NEW Skill 1: The Tech Phrasal Verb Decoder (`OUT • UP • OFF • DOWN`)
*Open **Tab 1 (`1. Phrasal Verb Decoder`)** in `lesson-06-sprint-lab.html` & paste **Canvas Block 2**.*

### 1. The Secret for Spanish Engineers (2 mins)
- Bárbara already knows formal Latin verbs (*deploy, resolve, eliminate, postpone, approve, reduce*) because they match Spanish (*desplegar, resolver, eliminar, posponer, aprobar, reducir*).
- But in daily English standups and Slack, native devs use **Phrasal Verbs 80% of the time**. Instead of memorizing random lists, group them by the **PARTICLE** (`OUT`, `UP`, `OFF`, `DOWN`):
  - **`OUT` (Expand outward / Eliminate):** **`roll out`** (deploy gradually), **`iron out`** (smooth out small bugs), **`rule out`** (eliminate a cause).
  - **`UP` (Make visible / Launch):** **`flag up`** (raise a risk early), **`spin up`** (launch a server/container fast), **`bring up`** (mention in a meeting).
  - **`OFF` (Start / Approve / Delay):** **`kick off`** (start a sprint/call), **`sign off on`** (officially approve), **`put off`** (postpone to next sprint).
  - **`DOWN` (Split / Funnel):** **`break down`** (split an epic into small tickets), **`narrow down`** (reduce 10 bug theories to 1).

### 2. The "Pronoun Sandwich" Drill (5 mins)
- **Rule:** When using `it` or `them`, put the pronoun **IN THE MIDDLE** (`roll it out` ✅, `iron them out` ✅, `narrow it down` ✅ — never *"roll out it"* ❌). *Exception:* 3-word phrasal verbs stay together (`sign off on it` ✅).
- **Live Upgrade Drill (6 mins):** Have Bárbara upgrade the 6 stiff "Latin/Spanish-style" engineering sentences on Tab 1 into natural phrasal-verb English out loud.

## 18–30 min | NEW Skill 2: The "BLUF" 45-Second Daily Standup & Bug Triage
*Switch to **Tab 2 (`2. 45s Standup & Triage`)** & paste **Canvas Block 3**.*
- **Why:** On Friday, Bárbara said her mind branches into too many tangents and she loses track of her original point.
- **The Solution — BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front):** In engineering meetings, never tell a chronological detective story. Use the **3-Bullet Standup Frame (45 Seconds Max)**:
  1. **Headline (10s):** *"Here's where we stand on [feature/bug]..."*
  2. **What We Ruled Out & Narrowed Down (20s):** *"We **ruled out** X and **narrowed it down** to Y. I'm **ironing out** the fix now."*
  3. **The Blocker / Ask (15s):** *"To **roll it out** on time, I just need [Name] to **sign off on** Z."*
- **Live Practice (3 Scenarios on Tab 2):**
  - **Card 1:** Mobile Checkout Dropdown Bug (CSS z-index / Safari).
  - **Card 2:** Slow Dashboard API Load (pagination vs. database query).
  - **Card 3:** Third-Party Auth Token Expiring Early.

### Round A: Stopping Friday Scope Creep (7 mins — Roleplay)
- **Scenario:** It's Thursday afternoon before a Monday release. Lewis plays an excited Product Manager who says:
  - *"Bárbara! Can we just add a dark-mode toggle and PDF export to the dashboard before Monday's rollout? It's just UI, right?"*
- **Bárbara's Goal (The "Yes, And / Trade-Off" Pushback Formula):**
  1. **Validate enthusiasm:** *"I can see why that would be great for users..."*
  2. **Flag up the hidden risk:** *"...however, I want to **flag up** a risk: adding PDF export now touches 4 components we've already tested."*
