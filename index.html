3 Friday Mock-Interview Warm-Up Prompts (December Frontend Referral Prep)
Stage 01 • HR & The 5-Year Vision

"Why This Referral & Where Do You See Yourself in 5 Years?"

Practice a 60-second answer connecting your friend's internal referral to why you chose to wait for their Junior Frontend role (rather than rushing into Pega), and how you plan to grow from Junior to Senior Frontend Engineer over the next 5 years.

Stage 02 • Technical JS & Angular (3-Point Rule)

"What Are the 3 Things the Interviewer Must Know?"

Pick one core Angular or JavaScript concept (e.g., Component vs. Service architecture, RxJS Observables, or TypeScript interfaces). Explain it in under 60 seconds using Louie's 3-Bullet Structure: (1) What it is in 1 sentence, (2) Why we use it, and (3) One real example from Sopra Steria or your DAW game.

Stage 03 • Team Fit & Creative Problem-Solving

Your Unfair Advantage: Communication & Empathy

As Louie highlighted at 74:55, technical teams love hiring engineers who are warm, communicative, and easy to work with. Prepare a 45-second story showing how you collaborate with teammates when you hit an unfamiliar technical problem.
