## 🎯 Teaching Philosophy for This Lesson
Bárbara is transitioning from 10+ years in artistic direction / production logistics into **Angular & TypeScript** software engineering. Her biggest unlock isn't technical knowledge—it's **owning her senior maturity** in English without apologizing or underselling her background.
- **Correction Rule:** Coach **structure and punchiness** first, small grammar tweaks second. When she undersells (*"I only have a little experience in..."*), stop and reframe it positively.

## ⏱️ Minute-by-Minute Teaching Script

### 1. Warm-Up & Nov 9 Polish Recap (00:00 – 07:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 1.*
- **What to say:** *"Before we dive into your pitch, let's do a 2-minute speed check on those four lines from Monday."*
- Have her read/complete them naturally in 1–2 casual sentences about her week:
  - *"I don't want **him** to start..."* (remind: object pronoun after `want`)
  - *"In my **hometown**..."* (not *born city*)
  - *"I'm really **itchy**"* (adjective for sensation)
  - ***sports center*** & ***five-a-side***
- **Quick check-in:** *"Did you get a chance to draft your Dream Job pitch, or are we building and polishing it live today?"*

### 2. Dream Job Pitch Review (07:00 – 23:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 2.*
- **Step A — First Take (3 mins):** Time her! Must stay **under 120 seconds**. Listen for:
  1. **Hook (0–15s):** Does she state her target role and value in the first two sentences?
  2. **Unfair Advantage (15–75s):** Does she frame her 10+ years of artistic direction/production logistics as *systems thinking, stakeholder management, and shipping under deadlines*—paired with **Angular & TypeScript**?
  3. **Confidence (75–120s):** Any apologetic hedging (*"just", "only", "still learning"*)?
- **Step B — Live Surgery (8 mins):** Put her opening 2 sentences on the Preply canvas and tighten them together. Replace filler with active verbs: *"I bring 10 years of shipping complex creative productions into building clean, strongly-typed Angular applications."*
- **Step C — Second Take (5 mins):** Have her deliver the tightened pitch again—aim for **85–100 seconds**, calm pace, strong downward ending.

### 3. Technical Interview Practice: STAR Method (23:00 – 43:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 3.*
- **How to teach STAR:** Remind her interviewers get lost when people spend 90 seconds on **Situation** and 10 seconds on **Action/Result**. Rule of thumb: **S (15%) → T (10%) → A (50%) → R (25%)**.
- **Target Vocabulary Check (2 mins):** Quickly verify she's comfortable using `trade-off`, `edge case`, `bottleneck`, `cross-functional`, and `refactor`.
- **Question 1 (9 mins):** *"Tell me about a time you had to refactor messy code or handle strict TypeScript types under a tight deadline."*
  - After she answers, ask one follow-up: *"What was the biggest **trade-off** you had to make, and what **edge case** did you catch?"*
- **Question 2 (9 mins):** *"How do you collaborate with designers or non-technical stakeholders when requirements change mid-sprint?"*
  - Coach her to highlight her **cross-functional** production background as a superpower for removing **bottlenecks**.

### 4. Wrap-Up & Homework (43:00 – 50:00)
*Paste Preply Canvas Block 4.*
- Share **3 strong phrases** she used + **2 gentle grammar polishes**.
- **Homework:** Record a polished **90-second STAR voice/video answer** to Question #1 or #2 (focusing on hitting the 50% **Action** + 25% **Result** split).

## 🛟 Pacing Rescue & Extension
- **If she hasn't prepared a pitch yet:** Spend 4 minutes co-writing a 3-bullet pitch outline together on the canvas first, then run Take 1 and Take 2.
- **If she finishes early:** Do a 60-second *"Why should we hire you?"* closing question where she uses **both** `cross-functional` and `trade-off`.
